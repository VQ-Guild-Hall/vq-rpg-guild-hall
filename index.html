Guild Hall

A private, personal automation project.

Guild Hall is a self-hosted AI assistant that helps its owner with research, briefings and organising information. It is not a public service and does not accept outside users.

The assistant may connect to the project's own Google account to read email, files and calendar entries for its owner. See the privacy policy for exactly how that data is handled.
